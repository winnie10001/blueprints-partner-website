Engagement 01

Brand Strategy & Identity

Introductory engagement
$1,500$599
Typical delivery: 3–4 weeks

We begin before the logo.

First we understand what you are building. Then we define how it should be understood and create the identity to carry that meaning.

  1. 01

    Clarify the business

    A detailed discovery questionnaire and a 60–90 minute strategy session covering the idea, ambition, commercial model, audience and competitive context.

    • Business and offer clarification
    • Audience definition
    • Current perception and ambition
    • Key strategic questions recorded
  2. 02

    Prepare the brand position

    We propose the clearest strategic direction for the brand and give the founder language that makes the business easier to understand.

    • Positioning and core brand message
    • One-line description and value proposition
    • About story and core offer language
    • Three name recommendations and one review round when naming is required
  3. 03

    Create the identity

    Two visual directions show how the strategy can become recognisable. The selected direction is developed through two consolidated revision rounds.

    • Two custom visual directions
    • Final logo suite
    • Colour palette and typography system
    • Supporting graphic language
  4. 04

    Prepare it for use

    The final system is organised so the founder and future collaborators know how to apply it consistently.

    • Practical brand guidelines
    • Organised final logo files
    • Core messaging summary
    • Final handover session
Best for

New ideas, founder-led businesses, creator offers and existing brands that no longer feel clear or credible.

Not included

Trademark clearance, legal naming approval, printing, photography, ongoing content production or unlimited revisions.

Before we begin

Useful questions.

Do I need to know which service I need?+

No. Explain the idea, pressure or result on the introductory call. We will recommend the smallest useful starting point.

How does payment work for Brand Strategy & Identity?+

The proposal confirms the payment schedule before work begins. The project starts once the first agreed payment and discovery information are received.

What happens after the Chief of Staff pilot?+

The pilot closes with a recommendation. You may stop there or discuss ongoing support, which currently starts from $2,000 per month depending on scope and capacity.

Will you use specialists on my project?+

Only where the work needs it. Their role, cost and responsibility are agreed with you before that work begins.

Do you guarantee every custom project?+

No. We accept custom builds only when the scope is suitable. Complex or specialist work may be separately scoped, delivered with an agreed partner or referred.

Terms / Plain English

How paid engagements work.

This page explains what you're agreeing to when you pay a deposit or fee to Blueprints Partner. It applies to Brand Strategy & Identity, the Shopify Starter build, the Fractional Chief of Staff pilot, and any custom-quoted project.

Scope

Every package has a defined scope, stated at the time of payment on the relevant page or email: what's included, what's not, and how many revision rounds are built in. That scope is what we build. Anything beyond it, including additional pages, products, features, or revision rounds, is treated as a separate custom quote, agreed and priced before we start on it.

Payment

Some packages are billed as a deposit that reserves your project start date, with the balance invoiced at handover. Others are billed in full upfront. Which applies is stated on the payment page itself before you pay. Paying confirms you've read and agreed to the scope for that specific package.

Cancellations and refunds

If you cancel before we've started work on your project, your payment is refunded in full minus the card processing fee. Once we've started work, deposits and payments are non-refundable, since that time and effort has already gone into your project. If something goes wrong on our end, tell us directly and we'll work it out with you.

Timelines

Stated project timelines (for example, 3-4 weeks for Brand Strategy & Identity) begin once your deposit or payment clears and, where relevant, once you've completed the discovery questionnaire. Delays on your side (late feedback, missing content or photos) can extend the timeline.

Ownership

Final deliverables (logos, brand guidelines, the built store, and similar) become yours once the project is paid in full. Work in progress, drafts, and unused concepts remain ours.

Questions

For anything about these terms, email info@blueprintspartner.com before you pay, not after.

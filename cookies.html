Cookies / Current use

Essential functions only.

Blueprints Partner does not currently load Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or another advertising or behavioural analytics tracker. Because no non-essential tracking cookies are used, the site does not show a consent banner.

What the site may use

The hosting and form-security services may use short-lived technical information needed to deliver the site, protect the enquiry endpoint, enforce rate limits and prevent duplicate or abusive requests. These functions are necessary to operate the site and are not used to build an advertising profile.

External services

If you choose “Book a call,” you leave this site and open Cal.com. If you choose “Send an email,” your email provider handles that action. Those services have their own privacy and cookie practices.

Future analytics

If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this notice will be updated and consent controls will be introduced before non-essential tools load.

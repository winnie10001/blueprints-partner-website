# Blueprints Partner Identity

## Position

Blueprints Partner brings strategy, branding and execution together for founders and creators. The identity combines editorial confidence with operational clarity.

## Mark

The symbol is formed from two interlocking architectural planes. They represent founders and creators, strategy and execution, and brand and business. The silver channel is the partnership: the structure that lets both sides move together.

Use the flat master for navigation, documents and small applications. Use the dimensional expression only where there is enough space for its material detail to remain controlled.

## Colour

- Blueprint Cobalt: `#1146D8`
- Signal Blue: `#2D67FF`
- Structural Navy: `#082B78`
- Brushed Silver: `#C7CEDA`
- Paper: `#F5F6F8`
- Ink: `#11151A`
- Warm Signal: `#F05A3A`

The warm signal is an accent, not a field colour. Cobalt should identify decisions, links and selected prices rather than wash every surface blue.

## Type

- Display: Instrument Serif. Use for major statements, editorial titles and selected pull quotes.
- Working type: Manrope. Use for navigation, body copy, labels, forms, prices and operational information.

Keep letter spacing at `0`. Let scale, weight, rules and whitespace establish hierarchy.

## Voice

Clear, assured and useful. Explain what changes, what is delivered and what happens next. Avoid inflated agency language, invented proof and generic AI claims.

Privacy / Plain English

How we handle your enquiry.

This notice explains how the preliminary enquiry form works.

What we collect

When you submit the preliminary enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, selected service, description of what you are building, the issue you want to move, your preferred timeline and any website or social link you choose to provide.

Why we use it

We use this information only to understand and respond to your enquiry, recommend a useful next step and protect the form from duplicate or abusive submissions. Submitting an enquiry does not add you to a marketing list.

How it is handled

Accepted enquiries are sent securely to info@blueprintspartner.com. The website stores only non-reversible technical hashes used to prevent duplicate and abusive submissions; it does not store the contents of your enquiry in a website database. We do not sell enquiry information.

Retention and deletion

Duplicate-protection hashes are retained for seven days. Short rate-limit records are automatically removed after one day. To ask about, correct or delete information you have sent us, contact info@blueprintspartner.com.

Contact

Blueprints Partner can be contacted about privacy at info@blueprintspartner.com. We do not publish a private home address. The appropriate legal operator and public address details will be added after review.
